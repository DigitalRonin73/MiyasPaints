FAQ

Good things to know before class

Do I need painting experience?

No. Classes are designed for beginners and guided step by step.

Are kids allowed?

Yes. Kids are welcome when the class theme and timing are a good fit.

How much is the class?

Classes are ¥4,500 per person unless a special event lists a different price.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held in Yomitan, Okinawa. Exact details are confirmed after reservation.

What should I bring?

All painting supplies are included. Wear something comfortable that can handle a little paint.

Can I book a private event?

Yes. Send a message with your preferred date, group size, and occasion.
