Before You Book

A few helpful notes

Cat-friendly home studio

There is a cat in the home. If you have cat allergies, please keep this in mind before booking.

Book at least 5 days ahead

Please make your reservation at least 5 days before the class so there is enough time to prepare materials.

Up to 6 guests

The studio is cozy and best for small groups, with a maximum of 6 guests per session.

Paint and clothing

Aprons are provided, but guests are responsible for any paint that gets on clothing or personal belongings. Please wear something relaxed and paint-friendly.

Arrive on time

Please arrive on time so everyone can start together and enjoy the full painting experience.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel your reservation, please message as early as possible so the seat and supplies can be adjusted.

FAQ

Good things to know before class

Is it really okay if I have no artistic talent?

Yes. We are not here to create perfect Picassos. You will be guided step by step, and the process gently brings out your creativity. By the end, you may surprise yourself with a painting you are proud of.

Can I join with my child?

Of course. Children are wonderful artists by nature, and they often show bolder, more beautiful color choices than adults. Please come with your family and enjoy a lively art time together.

How much is the class?

Classes are ¥4,500 per person unless a special event lists a different price.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held in Yomitan, Okinawa. Exact details are confirmed after reservation.

What if I am worried about getting paint on my clothes?

Cute aprons are available at the studio, but paint sometimes loves to jump in unexpected directions. Just in case, relaxed clothes that can handle a little paint are the best choice.

Can I add a paint party to my special event?

I'd love to help you celebrate! Let's plan a memorable paint party as a special activity for your birthday, anniversary, or gathering. Available for private mobile events at your home, cafe, or preferred venue. To secure your desired date and group size, please feel free to reach out as early as possible!
