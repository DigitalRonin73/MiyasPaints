Before You Book

A few helpful notes

Cat-friendly home studio

There is a cat in the home. If you have cat allergies, please keep this in mind before booking.

Up to 6 guests

The studio is cozy and best for small groups, with a maximum of 6 guests per session.

Paint and clothing

Aprons are provided, but guests are responsible for any paint that gets on clothing or personal belongings. Please wear something relaxed and paint-friendly.

FAQ

Before You Book

Is it really okay if I have no artistic talent?

Yes. We are not here to create perfect Picassos. You will be guided step by step, and the process gently brings out your creativity. By the end, you may surprise yourself with a painting you are proud of.

Can I join with my child?

Yes! Children are welcome to join. Many kids create amazing artwork, and painting together can be a fun family experience.

How much is the class?

Classes are ¥4,500 / $30 per person, unless a special event lists a different price.

How does payment work?

Payment details will be sent after your reservation is confirmed.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held in Yomitan, Okinawa. Exact details are confirmed after reservation.

What if I am worried about getting paint on my clothes?

Aprons are provided, but we recommend wearing clothes that you do not mind getting a little paint on.

Do you offer private events or mobile workshops?

Yes! I’d love to help make your gathering extra special. I can bring all painting supplies to your location for birthdays, family celebrations, private gatherings, and small group experiences. If you already have a venue in mind, simply let me know your preferred date, location, and group size, and we’ll create something memorable together.
